About the shops

Four stores on Main Street, each with a different focus.

Mountain Style Shops runs four retail locations in downtown Breckenridge. Mountain Style and Mountain Vibe lean toward apparel and accessories. Colorado Concept focuses on state-themed gifts. Simply Breck carries Breckenridge-specific merchandise.

  • Walkable downtownAll four addresses are on or just off Main Street — easy to hit in one afternoon.
  • Year-round inventorySeasonal apparel, Colorado gifts, and souvenirs stocked throughout the year.
